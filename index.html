What I'm building · Launching Summer 2027

LAYERED HEALTH

Self-Discovery, Layer by Layer.

Layered Health is the private practice I'm opening in Summer 2027. I believe we're all complex, layered people, and that therapy can be a place where we have room to explore those layers, understand ourselves more fully, and heal and grow from what we find there.

Layer one

Explore

We look at what's there, the parts of you that are loud right now and the ones that have been quiet for a long time.

Layer two

Discern

Then we look underneath it, noticing the patterns, where they came from, and what they've been protecting.

Layer three

Integrate

We bring what we find back into your life, so insight becomes something you can actually live with and use.

What I'll offer

Individual Therapy and Relationship Therapy, including for consensually non-monogamous relationships.

Who it's for

LGBTQ+ and neurodivergent people, and members of other historically marginalized communities. Come as you are. All of you.

When

Summer 2027, after I graduate from Penn and become an LAPC. Follow along on Instagram at @layeredhealth.
