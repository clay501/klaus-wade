Why work with me

We are more than one thing.

One feeling. One story.
We are layered.

I approach therapy with the recognition that we're all complex, layered people, and that through therapy we can come to understand ourselves, heal, and grow.

I'll meet you with non-judgment and together we'll create a space to collaborate in, exploring your lived experiences and perceptions, discern the meaning within them, and integrate what you learn to live a more fulfilling life.

Who I work with

Teens and adults ages 14+ for individual and relationship therapy

In particular, I offer affirming, identity-informed care for LGBTQ+ and neurodivergent people, people of color, and members of all historically marginalized communities.

I recognize the many ways that our identities intersect and overlap, and acknowledge that culture, history, and society shape how we understand ourselves and how the world perceives us.

LGBTQ+ Affirming

Care that recognizes, considers, and celebrates queer sexual and gender identities.

Neurodivergent Affirming

Care that's inclusive of both diagnosed and not diagnosed people that recognizes that some people's brains process, learn, and behave in a way that's different from what society considers typical.

Cultural Humility & Responsiveness

Care that recognizes each person as the expert of their own identity and culture, meets the unfamiliar with curiosity, and aligns with their values.

My therapeutic process

Explore. Discern. Integrate.

01

Explore

We look at what's there: the feelings, stories and patterns you bring into the room.

02

Discern

Then we look underneath it, and notice what each layer is holding and why.

03

Integrate

You bring what you find back into your life, so you can heal and grow.

Sunflower Radiance Counseling · Glenolden, PA

In-Person & Virtual Therapy in PA

At Sunflower Radiance Counseling, I see clients virtually throughout Pennsylvania and in person at our office in Glenolden, in Delaware County. Sunflower Radiance is an LGBTQ+ and neurodivergent-affirming, identity-informed practice, and I'm supervised there by its owner, Shannon Kilroy, LPC.

Our identities intersect and overlap in so many ways, and the culture, history, and society we come from shape how we understand ourselves and the world around us, so all of that belongs in the room, too.
